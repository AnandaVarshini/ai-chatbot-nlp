Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the AI Study Assistant ("the Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our chatbot.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the Service, we collect:

  • Account information: username, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Conversation data: the questions you submit and the responses returned by the chatbot, stored against your account.
  • Usage analytics: aggregate counts of queries, intents, and answer rates. These are visible only to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the chatbot service.
  • To display your conversation history and analytics.
  • To improve the relevance of responses over time.

3. Passwords and Security

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage. We never store plaintext passwords. Authentication uses JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with signed expiry.

4. External Services

When a query cannot be answered from the local knowledge base, the Service may forward the query text to Wikipedia or DuckDuckGo to retrieve a public answer. These services receive only the raw query string, not your username or email.

5. Data Retention

Your conversation history is retained until you delete individual entries from the History page or clear your history in full. Account deletion is not currently self-service; contact the operator to remove an account.

6. Your Rights

  • You can view all your stored conversation data on the History page.
  • You can delete individual conversations or clear the entire history at any time.
  • You can log out at any time, which removes your session token from the browser.

7. Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact the project team through the repository or the email listed in the Terms of Service.
